Rails Girls San Francisco November 12th, 2026

Hello world!

Build your first Rails app with a coding agent. In this free one-day workshop you'll make a small tool for yourself, your team, family or friends.

Register on Luma. Spots are limited by the number of mentors.

You learn to build a real app: you describe what you want, the coding agent writes the code, and your mentor helps you understand what it built.

You need a laptop and charger. You don't need any coding experience.

Want to help? Ruby engineers can mentor a group. Email us.

FAQ

How much does the workshop cost? Nothing, it's free!

Who is this aimed for? The event is open for all, but we focus on women and nonbinary people, and especially those in non-technical roles at Ruby companies: product, design, marketing, sales, support, ops, recruiting. Everyone curious is welcome.

What will I work with? You work in a small group with a mentor who writes Ruby for a living. Tell us your role and, optionally, what you'd like to build when you register.
